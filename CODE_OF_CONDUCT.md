# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in the AstroArabia community respectful, welcoming, and productive for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or skill level.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate in issues, pull requests, discussions, and reviews.
- Welcome different perspectives and help others learn.
- Give feedback about code and ideas, not people.
- Respect privacy and do not share personal information without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery in community spaces.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or repeated bad-faith behavior.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and comments that violate this Code of Conduct. Serious or repeated violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainers. Do not open a public issue for a sensitive report.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces and when representing AstroArabia in public contexts.
